## About

BMWholic is a BMW enthusiast website built to celebrate the heritage, engineering, performance, and design of BMW automobiles.

From iconic classics to modern performance machines, the platform brings together BMW's history, model catalogue, M Performance heritage, and automotive culture through an interactive web experience.

Designed as a personal archive of Bavarian engineering, BMWholic combines automotive storytelling, detailed vehicle information, custom 3D animations, and immersive visual elements to explore what makes BMW more than just a machine.

## Features

- **Cinematic Introduction:** A video-based opening experience with sound controls, introducing visitors to the world of BMWholic.
- **BMW Model Catalogue:** Explore BMW vehicles across different eras, series, families, and generations.
- **Detailed Model Information:** Discover vehicle specifications, engine configurations, performance figures, drivetrain details, historical backgrounds, and engineering features.
- **Model Comparison:** Compare different BMW models and explore their specifications side by side.
- **BMW History:** Explore BMW's journey through historical milestones, logo evolution, engineering developments, and motorsport heritage.
- **M Performance:** Discover BMW M's performance legacy, featuring the M4, its generations, engineering details, and motorsport connections.
- **Interactive 3D Animations:** Experience custom-built 3D automotive visuals, including vehicle scenes, instrument clusters, engine-inspired visuals, and other animated elements.
- **3D Model Viewer:** Explore supported BMW 3D models through an interactive viewing experience.
- **Comfort / Sport / Track Modes:** Switch between three visual modes that influence the website's animations, colours, and overall experience.
- **BMW Gallery:** Browse automotive visuals through category filters and an interactive image viewer.
- **BMW Knowledge Assistant:** Explore BMW-related information through an integrated conversational interface.
- **BMW Personality Quiz:** Discover which BMW model matches your preferences through an interactive quiz.
- **Interactive User Interface:** Responsive navigation, animated elements, dynamic content, and interactive components throughout the website.
